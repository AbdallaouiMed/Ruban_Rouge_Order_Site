# Image credits and status

**These are placeholder photographs for the demo. They are not photos of Ruban Rouge's own products.**
Replace them with the bakery's own photography before launch: upload from the admin (*Produits et prix*, *Images du site*), or set `image` on each product in `src/data/bakery.ts` and change the defaults in `src/data/demo.ts`.

Every file here was cropped to its slot, resized and given one consistent warm colour grade (`scratchpad` script: soft-light warm overlay, slight contrast and saturation lift, soft vignette), then exported as WebP.
